# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be respectful in language and tone
- Give actionable technical feedback
- Assume good intent and discuss issues in good faith
- Focus on ideas, code quality, and outcomes

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Trolling, intimidation, or doxxing
- Repeated disruptive behavior in issues or pull requests

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, commits, code, and other contributions that violate this policy.
